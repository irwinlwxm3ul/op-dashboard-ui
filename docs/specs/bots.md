# Module spec: bots

Owned paths: src/shared/bots/** and tests/bots.test.ts

- MODULE: Bot AI (src/shared/bots): createBotDriver(sim: SimView): BotDriver and botName(rng) per ARCHITECTURE.md. Bots are players with isBot === true; they act ONLY through sim.queueInput with PlayerInput (same validation as humans, no cheating: no reading other players inventories/hp beyond what a human could see; perception must use line of sight and view cones). GameSim/MatchRunner are written concurrently: rely only on the SimView contract (src/shared/sim/view.ts) and Stage A modules.

- Per bot state: seq counter, current goal, path, perception memory, reaction timers. Input each tick: seq++, rt = sim.time*1000, base yaw/pitch (bots must compensate server-side recoil partially by reading their own player.recoilPitch/recoilYaw — compensation factor by difficulty), buttons, stance, slot, actions.

- Difficulty (config.botDifficulty): reaction time (easy ~0.8 s, normal ~0.45 s, hard ~0.25 s), aim error that converges while tracking (easy large), max turn rate, vision range (easy 150 m, normal 250 m, hard 400 m), recoil compensation (easy 30 percent, normal 60, hard 85), burst discipline, use of cover/strafing, healing intelligence. Bots must be beatable and fun: they should not one-tap through foliage or snap instantly.

- Behaviour (hierarchical state machine with utility scoring, think at ~6 Hz staggered by id; steering every tick): PLANE: choose a landing spot (weighted random town/loot-rich area, squad members share the leader target; human-led squads follow the human), jump when the plane is near the closest point to it (with randomness), steer freefall toward target, open chute (or rely on auto); LOOT: pick up useful loot nearby (a weapon if none or better tier, matching ammo, armor/helmet upgrades, backpack, heals when low stock, energy, frags) via action pickup when within INTERACT_RANGE, explore buildings (loot spawns are mostly indoors) — move to the nearest unexplored building; ZONE: when outside the next circle or the zone timer is low relative to travel time, rotate toward the safe zone (target point inside next circle with margin), preferring a nearby vehicle for long rotations (> 250 m) — enter as driver (action enter) and drive with throttle/steer toward the target, avoid obstacles roughly, exit (action exit) when close or stuck; squadmates may take passenger seats; COMBAT: perceive enemies (view cone ~140 degrees + LOS raycasts with a global per-tick budget, hearing via sim.noises to investigate), react after reaction time, aim at center mass/head with error, ADS beyond ~25 m, fire full-auto at close range, bursts at mid range, taps/singles at long range, lead moving targets (ballistic weapons: use items simulateTrajectory/muzzle velocity to compute lead and drop), strafe while shooting, crouch at range, seek cover when health low (move behind nearest obstacle away from enemy), reload when empty and out of contact, switch weapon by range, throw frags at enemies behind cover occasionally, finish downed enemies; SUPPORT: heal when health < 60 and no recent contact (best item for the situation), use energy drinks, revive downed teammates (go to them, action revive, hold INTERACT, stand still) when safe; squad bots in a human team follow the human (stay within 10-30 m, defend, revive them); IDLE: wander toward points of interest inside the zone.

- Navigation: build a lazy ground-level occupancy grid (1 m cells, generated per 64 m tile on demand from world boxes/trees/rocks whose vertical span blocks a standing character at terrain height, plus water and steep slopes) and run A* (with a per-tick node budget; path cache; smoothing via line-of-walk checks) for destinations, so bots walk through doors into buildings and around obstacles. Local steering with feelers; stuck detection (little progress for 1.5 s) -> jump / sidestep / repath / give up target. Bots in buildings may use stairs opportunistically but ground-floor navigation is enough.

- Performance: 60 bots must cost < 3 ms per tick on average in Node (global budgets for LOS raycasts and A* expansions per tick, staggered thinking, scratch objects; no per-tick allocations in hot paths).

- botName(rng): original gamer tags (no real people or trademarks), e.g. combinations of adjectives/nouns/numbers.

- Tests (tests/bots.test.ts): build a minimal mock SimView backed by real generateWorld(seed, kind) from src/shared/world plus hand-made PlayerState objects (use items emptyInventory, etc.) and a queueInput that records inputs. Test: during plane phase a bot eventually sends the jump action near its target; a bot next to loot sends a pickup action with that loot id; a bot with a weapon and a visible enemy in front turns toward and fires after its reaction time but not before; it does not see an enemy behind a wall; low health with no enemies -> use action with a heal item; outside the zone -> moves toward the zone center; A* path from outside a building to a loot spawn inside goes through a door gap (path cells never inside boxes); timing: driver.update for 60 bots averages under budget.
