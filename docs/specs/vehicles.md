# Module spec: vehicles

Owned paths: src/shared/vehicles/** and tests/vehicles.test.ts

- MODULE: Vehicles (src/shared/vehicles) — exact API in ARCHITECTURE.md. Arcade but believable physics that feels good to drive: grip, drift with handbrake, weight transfer feel via pitch/roll, airtime off ramps/hills, solid collisions.

- Do NOT import src/shared/physics (written concurrently). Implement your own collision vs world using World.queryBoxes/queryTrees/queryRocks and heightAt/groundAt (types.ts World interface). For tests, build a small fake World inside the test file (heightmap + boxes + trees + rocks with simple query implementations).

- Vehicle types: buggy (2 seats, fast and agile, maxSpeed ~33 m/s, health 1000), uaz (4 seats, sturdy, ~29 m/s, health 1600, heavier), bike (2 seats, fastest ~38 m/s, fragile health 600, narrow, lean/roll into turns visually via roll). Seat 0 = driver. Seat offsets in local space (-Z forward). seatCanShoot: driver false; passengers true (bike rear passenger true). fuelMax 100; fuelPerSec at full throttle tuned so a full tank lasts ~5-7 minutes of full throttle; idle consumes nothing; no fuel = no drive force.

- stepVehicle(v, ctl, dt, world, others, out): sub-step (<= 1/120 s per step or as needed). Engine force along heading toward maxSpeed (boost +15 percent speed/accel while boost held, consumes fuel 2x), brake/reverse when throttle opposite to motion, coasting drag, rolling resistance. Steering: steer eases toward ctl.steer at steerSpeed, reduced at high speed; yawRate from bicycle model (speed / wheelBase * tan(steer*steerMax)), lateral velocity damped by grip (handbrake reduces rear grip -> drift). Ground: sample ground height (terrain via heightAt, plus box tops via world.groundAt or queryBoxes) under 4 wheel points (2 for bike), suspension spring-damper toward ride height, pitch/roll from wheel heights (smoothed); bike roll from lean into turns. Gravity component along slope affects speed. Airborne when wheels far above ground: ballistic with gravity, keep orientation smoothing; landing impact above ~8 m/s vertical counts as crash. Collisions: the OBB (halfExtents, yaw) vs world AABBs and tree/rock cylinders — approximate with 2-3 circles along the length for cars and 2 for bike, resolve penetration, reflect/kill velocity along normal with restitution ~0.2, compute impact = velocity change along normal; vehicle-vs-vehicle circle sets with mass-weighted impulse (apply to both, report crash for this one). out.crash = largest impact this step (m/s), crashPos. Water: if water depth at center > 1.0 m set inWater and heavily damp velocity, engine off. Map bounds clamp. Destroyed vehicles: no control, friction stops them. rpm: derived from speed & throttle for engine sound (idle ~900, max ~6500) stored in v.rpm. Update v.throttle, v.steer, v.speed (signed forward), v.onGround, v.yawRate.

- createVehicle(id, spawn, fuelFraction=0.6): positioned on ground at spawn, yaw from spawn. crashDamage(impact, type): vehicle damage grows superlinearly above ~6 m/s impact (bike more fragile), occupants damage starts above ~10 m/s (a 30 m/s head-on wall crash should nearly kill unarmored occupants but a 12 m/s bump does a little). seatWorldPosition, exitPosition (to the side of the seat, falling back to other side/behind/on top, using world collision to ensure a free spot at ground height), raycastVehicle (ray vs oriented box from yaw/pitch/roll; returns distance or -1), vehicleHitsPoint (point within OBB inflated by radius, used for run-over).

- Tests (tests/vehicles.test.ts): accelerates to near maxSpeed on flat ground and never exceeds it without boost; brakes; reverses; turns (yaw changes sign correctly: steer right => yaw decreases, matching math.ts convention positive yaw = left turn); handbrake induces drift (lateral velocity larger); fuel decreases with throttle and stops movement at 0; collision with a wall stops the vehicle and reports crash impact ~speed; vehicle does not pass through walls/trees at max speed; vehicles collide with each other; goes airborne off a ramp/cliff and lands; water stops vehicle; crashDamage monotonic; exitPosition is free of colliders; raycastVehicle hits/misses correctly with rotation; determinism.
