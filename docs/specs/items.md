# Module spec: items

Owned paths: src/shared/items/** and tests/items.test.ts

- MODULE: Items, combat math, ballistics, inventory and loot tables (src/shared/items) — exact API in ARCHITECTURE.md, PLUS add: export function launchVelocity(w: WeaponDef, dir: Vec3, out?: Vec3): Vec3 (muzzle velocity along dir rotated upward by zeroAngle(w, 100) for ballistic weapons so the sight is zeroed at 100 m).

- Weapon tuning (PUBG-like, must feel distinct and fair): pistol (9mm, mag 15, semi, ~400 rpm, dmg ~35), smg (9mm, mag 30, auto, ~750 rpm, dmg ~33, great hip fire, short falloff), ar (5.56, mag 30, auto, ~690 rpm, dmg ~41, red dot, controllable climbing recoil), shotgun (12 gauge, pump, mag 5, 9 pellets ~24 dmg each, shell-by-shell reload via reloadPerShell, short range falloff), dmr (7.62, semi, mag 10, ~ 300 rpm, dmg ~52, ballistic hitscan=false, muzzleVelocity ~850, 4x scope), sniper (ammo_300, bolt, mag 5, ~40 rpm, dmg ~80, ballistic, muzzleVelocity ~760, 8x scope). Head multipliers ~2.0-2.5 (sniper 2.5), limb ~0.9. Hitscan weapons still have muzzleVelocity for tracer speed. ADS times 0.15-0.35 s, draw times 0.4-0.9 s, reload times realistic (tactical faster than empty). adsZoom: iron/reddot ~0.8 (slight zoom), x4 0.25, x8 0.125. moveSpeedMul 0.85-1.0. loudness: pistol 250 m, smg 300, ar 450, shotgun 350, dmr 600, sniper 800. KickDef tuned per weapon for satisfying cosmetic kick (sniper big camera punch, smg small rapid). lootWeight rarer for sniper/dmr.

- Ammo stacks, consumables: bandage (4 s, +10 cap 75, move while using 0.8), firstaid (6 s, sets to 75 cap 75, move 0.6), medkit (8 s, to 100, move 0.4), energy (4 s, +40 boost), fuel jerry can (5 s, effect handled by sim; applyConsumable must not touch health for fuel). Throwable frag: fuse 4 s, damage 120 falloff over radius 7 m, throwSpeed ~18. Gear: vest1/2/3 reduction 0.30/0.40/0.55 durability 200/220/250 capacity +50; helmet1/2/3 reduction 0.30/0.40/0.55 durability 80/150/230; pack1/2/3 capacity +100/+150/+200. Base capacity 50. Item weights per PLAN-like values (ammo small per round, medkit 20, firstaid 10, bandage 2, energy 4, fuel 20, frag 12). Weapons do not use capacity (they occupy slots).

- Spread: spreadCone combines hip/ADS blend by ctx.ads, movement (scaled by speed/6.3), airborne penalty, stance (crouch 0.8, prone 0.6), bloom (clamped). shotDirections: deterministic from seed (hash-based, use rng.ts hash32/hashFloat), uniform within cone (disk sampling), shotgun pellets spread with pelletSpread around the shot direction plus cone.

- Recoil (server-authoritative, learnable): recoilForShot(w, sprayIndex, seed, stance, ads): vertical = w.recoil.vertical * min(verticalMaxMul, 1 + verticalGrowth*sprayIndex) (firstShotMul on index 0); horizontal follows a deterministic pattern that depends only on weapon id and sprayIndex (a smooth seeded walk so the same weapon always drifts the same way = learnable) plus small per-shot jitter from seed (<= 25 percent of horizontal); multiply by adsMul blended with ads, crouchMul/proneMul by stance. recoverRecoil: when not firing, move both toward 0 at w.recoil.recovery rad/s (exponential-ish ease so it settles smoothly); when firing, no recovery. With null weapon recover quickly.

- Damage: computeDamage(weapon, part, distance, armor): base * part multiplier * falloff(distance); head reduced by helmet (if helmet > 0 and helmetHp > 0), body by vest, limbs not reduced; armor durability damage = the pre-reduction damage; armorBroke when it reaches 0 (then level becomes 0 in applyArmorDamage). Shotgun computeDamage is per pellet.

- Ballistics: stepBallistic integrates gravity*gravityScale and linear drag (semi-implicit Euler is fine but must be stable for dt up to 1/30 — sub-step internally to <= 1/120 s). simulateTrajectory(w, range) returns time of flight and drop relative to the initial straight line (unzeroed) by numerical integration; zeroAngle(w, r) solves the elevation that hits the sight line at r (bisection). Expected: sniper ~0.4 s and noticeable drop at 300 m, DMR flatter.

- Inventory (mutating PlayerState from types.ts): capacity, usedCapacity, roomFor, pickupItem (weapons: fill empty primary slot A then B for primaries, pistol slot for pistol; if full, replace the ACTIVE primary slot (or slot A if active is not primary) and return the old weapon in dropped (with its mag); gear: equip if slot empty or different level — replaced gear goes to dropped; packs change capacity; ammo/consumables/throwables limited by remaining capacity, take what fits), removeItem, applyConsumable (heal/boost per defs, not fuel), tickBoost (boost decays ~1 point every 2.5 s, heals over time: >=60 boost: 1.0 hp/s, >=20: 0.6 hp/s, >0: 0.3 hp/s, never above 100, only when alive), boostSpeedMul (1.04 at >=60 else 1), emptyInventory.

- Loot tables rollLoot(rng, tier): returns 1-3 item stacks: weapons (with matching ammo stack of 1-2 mags worth and mag filled to 0..magSize), ammo stacks, consumables, gear with level weighted by tier (tier 2 = military: more level 2-3 gear and DMR/sniper), frags, fuel cans occasionally. All counts sensible.

- Tests (tests/items.test.ts): every ItemId has a def; weapon defs sane; damage numbers (e.g. AR body no armor ~41, with vest2 ~24.6; sniper head vs helmet2 one-shot >= 100); falloff; armor breaks; spread determinism and cone bound; recoil deterministic, climbs, learnable horizontal pattern identical across different seeds except jitter; recovery reaches ~0; ballistics: sniper 300 m time ~0.39-0.45 s and drop between 0.5 and 1.2 m, zeroAngle makes trajectory cross sight line at 100 m within 2 cm; inventory pickup/replace rules and capacity; consumable effects; boost; rollLoot produces valid items for each tier deterministically.
